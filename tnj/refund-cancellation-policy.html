Return & Refund Policy

Last updated: August 28, 2026

Note to clinic staff: a few operational details in this policy (notice period for cancellations, cancellation charges, number of allowed reschedules, refund processing days, product return window) are marked TO CONFIRM below. Please share the exact figures so this page can be finalised.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Advanced GroHair & GloSkin – Thanjavur.

We are a hair and skin clinic offering consultations and aesthetic and clinical treatment services. This Return and Refund Policy explains how payments, cancellations, rescheduling and refunds are handled for services booked at our clinic or through our website, https://adgrohairgloskinthanjavur.com/.

We believe in transparency. This policy is written in plain language so that you understand clearly, before you pay, what is refundable and what is not.

By booking a consultation, purchasing a treatment, enrolling in a treatment package, or making any payment to Advanced GroHair & GloSkin – Thanjavur, you confirm that you have read, understood and accepted the terms set out below.

Please note: Our services are personal, professional healthcare and aesthetic services delivered by trained practitioners. Unlike physical goods, a service that has already been performed cannot be returned. For this reason, this policy distinguishes carefully between payments made in advance and payments made for services already delivered.

2. Consultation and Appointment Fees

  • Consultation fees cover the professional time of our doctors, trichologists, dermatology consultants and clinical staff, along with any diagnostic assessment carried out during your visit.
  • Consultation fees become non-refundable once the consultation has taken place, regardless of whether you choose to proceed with any recommended treatment.
  • If you cancel or reschedule a consultation within the notice period described in Section 6, your consultation fee may be adjusted against a future appointment or refunded, subject to review.
  • If you do not attend a booked consultation without informing the clinic in advance (a "no-show"), the consultation fee may be forfeited.
  • Where the clinic offers a complimentary or promotional consultation, no refund arises, as no fee has been collected.
  • In some cases, at the clinic's discretion, the consultation fee may be adjusted against the cost of a treatment you subsequently book. Where such an adjustment is offered, it will be confirmed to you in writing at the time of booking.

3. Treatment and Service Payments

  • All treatment charges are communicated to you before the treatment begins. You will be informed of the applicable cost, the number of sessions advised, and the payment terms.
  • Payment may be collected in full in advance, in instalments, or on a per-session basis, depending on the treatment plan agreed with you.
  • We accept payments through the modes made available at our clinic and, where applicable, through our website. All payments are in Indian Rupees (₹) and are subject to applicable taxes.
  • A payment receipt or invoice will be issued for every payment received. Please retain your receipt, as it is required for processing any refund request.
  • Once a treatment session has been performed, the amount paid for that specific session is treated as consumed and is not refundable.
  • Where a treatment plan has been paid for in advance and only part of it has been used, refund eligibility for the unused portion is dealt with under Section 4 and Section 8.

4. Refund Eligibility

Refunds are considered on a case-by-case basis. You may be eligible to request a refund in situations including, but not limited to:

  • Services not yet availed – you have paid in advance for a treatment or session that has not yet been performed.
  • Cancellation by the clinic – the clinic cancels a scheduled treatment and is unable to offer you a mutually acceptable alternative date.
  • Medical unsuitability – following clinical assessment, our practitioners advise that a paid treatment is not suitable for you and should not proceed (see Section 9).
  • Duplicate or excess payment – a payment has been collected twice, or an amount higher than the agreed charge has been debited.
  • Payment or technical error – an online transaction has been processed incorrectly.

How to request a refund

  1. Submit your request in writing to the clinic, either in person at our reception or through the contact details given in Section 13.
  2. Include your full name, registered mobile number, treatment or service details, date of payment, amount paid, and your reason for the request.
  3. Attach or produce your original payment receipt or invoice.

Review process

  • Every refund request is reviewed by the clinic management along with the treating practitioner, where clinically relevant.
  • The review takes into account the nature of the service, the number of sessions already delivered, consumables and clinical materials already used or reserved for you, the notice given, and any third-party or payment-gateway costs already incurred.
  • All refunds, where applicable, are subject to review based on the specific circumstances of the service or treatment concerned. Approval of a refund in one instance does not create an entitlement in any other instance.
  • The outcome of the review will be communicated to you in writing.

5. Non-Refundable Services

The following are generally not eligible for refund:

  • Consultations, assessments, diagnostic tests and evaluations that have already been carried out.
  • Treatment sessions that have already been performed, whether in full or in part.
  • Services where single-use consumables, medications, injectables, or personalised clinical materials have already been opened, prepared or used for you.
  • Customised or personalised treatment protocols and formulations prepared specifically for you.
  • Promotional offers, discounted packages, seasonal camps or festival offers that are expressly stated to be non-refundable at the time of purchase.
  • Complimentary, free or bundled sessions provided as part of an offer or package.
  • Gift vouchers, prepaid cards and referral credits, unless expressly stated otherwise at the time of issue.
  • Appointments missed without prior intimation ("no-shows").
  • Sessions lapsed due to non-attendance beyond the validity period of a package (see Section 8).
  • Dissatisfaction based solely on the pace, extent or visibility of results, where the treatment has been delivered as agreed and in accordance with accepted clinical practice.
  • Discontinuation of a treatment course by the client for personal reasons, in respect of the sessions already completed.

A note on results. Hair and skin treatments are biological in nature. Outcomes depend on individual factors and cannot be guaranteed. Please read Section 9 carefully. Payment for our services is payment for professional clinical care, expertise, time and materials – not for a specific or assured result.

6. Cancellation and Rescheduling Policy

We understand that plans change. We ask only that you inform us early, so that your appointment slot can be offered to another client.

Cancellation or rescheduling by you

  • Please inform the clinic at least TO CONFIRM – notice period (e.g. 24 / 48 hours) before your scheduled appointment.
  • Notice may be given by telephone, WhatsApp, email, or in person at our reception.
  • Where adequate notice is given, we will make every reasonable effort to reschedule your appointment to a date and time convenient to you, at no additional cost.
  • Where notice is given late, or where you do not attend without informing us, a cancellation or administrative charge of TO CONFIRM – charge or percentage may apply, or the session may be treated as consumed.
  • Repeated late cancellations or no-shows may result in the clinic requiring advance confirmation or advance payment for future bookings.

Rescheduling limits

  • An appointment may ordinarily be rescheduled up to TO CONFIRM – number of times. Beyond this, the clinic may require a fresh booking.
  • Rescheduling is always subject to the availability of the treating practitioner and clinic slots.

Cancellation by the clinic

We may occasionally need to cancel or postpone an appointment – for example, due to practitioner unavailability, equipment maintenance, power failure, or other operational reasons. In such cases:

  • You will be informed at the earliest possible opportunity.
  • You will be offered a rescheduled appointment at your convenience, at no additional cost.
  • If a suitable alternative cannot be arranged, the amount paid for that unperformed session will be refunded or retained as clinic credit, as you prefer.

7. Refund Processing Time

  • Approved refund requests are ordinarily processed within TO CONFIRM – number of working days from the date of written approval.
  • Refunds are made only to the original mode and source of payment. For example, a payment made by card will be refunded to the same card; a UPI payment will be refunded to the same UPI account.
  • Where the original payment mode is no longer available or cannot be used, an alternative mode will be arranged after verification of your identity and payment records.
  • Once processed from our end, the credit of funds to your account depends on your bank, card issuer or payment gateway. This typically takes an additional TO CONFIRM – number of working days, and this timeline is outside the clinic's control.
  • Applicable taxes, statutory deductions, payment-gateway charges and bank charges may be deducted from the refund amount where these have already been incurred and are non-recoverable by the clinic.
  • You will be provided with a refund confirmation or acknowledgement once the refund has been initiated.

8. Treatment Packages and Advance Payments

Many of our hair and skin treatments are delivered as multi-session packages. The following terms apply to packages and advance payments:

  • Package pricing is offered at a concessional rate on the understanding that the full course will be completed. This discount is the reason package terms differ from single-session terms.
  • Each package has a validity period, which will be communicated to you at the time of purchase. Sessions not availed within the validity period will lapse and will not be refundable, unless an extension has been approved in writing by the clinic.
  • If you choose to discontinue a package mid-way:
    • Sessions already availed will be charged at the applicable single-session (non-package) rate, not the discounted package rate.
    • Any complimentary or bonus sessions already used will also be charged at the single-session rate.
    • The balance amount, if any, may be considered for refund or converted to clinic credit, subject to review and to a deduction of TO CONFIRM – administrative charge.
  • Advance payments made to block or reserve appointment slots, equipment time or imported consumables may be partly or wholly non-refundable, as such costs are committed by the clinic in advance. The applicable terms will be explained to you before you make the payment.
  • Packages are non-transferable to another person unless expressly approved in writing by the clinic management.
  • Where a package is discontinued on the written advice of our treating practitioner for medical reasons, refund of the unused portion will be considered on a pro-rata basis under Section 9.
  • Where a package is paid for in instalments and is discontinued, the clinic reserves the right to recover the value of sessions already delivered at single-session rates before processing any balance.

9. Medical or Treatment Suitability

Your safety comes before your treatment plan. Please read this section carefully.

  • All treatments are provided only after a clinical assessment of your suitability by our qualified practitioners.
  • You are requested to disclose complete and accurate information about your medical history, existing conditions, allergies, current medications, pregnancy or planned pregnancy, and any previous treatments. Refunds may be affected where a material fact has not been disclosed to us.
  • If, during assessment or at any point during a treatment course, our practitioners determine that a treatment is not medically suitable for you or should be discontinued, we will inform you and stop the treatment. In such cases, the amount paid for the unperformed portion will be considered for refund or clinic credit, on a pro-rata basis.
  • Individual results vary. Response to hair and skin treatments depends on factors including age, genetics, hormonal status, underlying medical conditions, skin and scalp type, lifestyle, nutrition, adherence to the advised protocol, and aftercare compliance.
  • No specific outcome, timeline, or degree of improvement is guaranteed or promised, either verbally or in writing, by the clinic or any of its staff. Any images, testimonials or case references shared are illustrative of individual experiences and are not a representation of the results you will obtain.
  • Certain treatments may produce temporary side effects such as redness, mild swelling, dryness, irritation or sensitivity. These are recognised and expected responses in clinical practice and do not, by themselves, constitute grounds for a refund.
  • Following the aftercare instructions, prescribed medication schedule and review appointments advised by our practitioners is essential. Refund requests arising from non-adherence to advised protocols will not ordinarily be accepted.
  • If you experience any unexpected reaction or discomfort, please contact the clinic immediately so that we can review and support you clinically.

10. Products, If Applicable

Where the clinic sells or dispenses hair care, skin care or supplementary products:

  • Products may be returned or exchanged only if they are unopened, unused, and in their original sealed packaging, with the original invoice, and within TO CONFIRM – number of days of purchase.
  • For reasons of hygiene, safety and regulatory compliance, opened or used products cannot be returned or exchanged.
  • Prescription medicines and dispensed pharmaceutical items are non-returnable and non-refundable, in line with standard practice for medicinal products.
  • If a product is received in a damaged condition, or is found to have expired or to be defective at the time of purchase, please report this to the clinic within TO CONFIRM – number of days. On verification, we will replace the product or process a refund.
  • Products purchased under a promotional offer, combo or bundled scheme may carry different return terms, which will be stated at the point of sale.
  • Product returns, where accepted, are refunded to the original payment mode in accordance with Section 7.

11. Exceptional Circumstances

We recognise that life does not always follow a schedule. The clinic may, at its sole discretion, relax the terms of this policy in genuine and exceptional situations, such as:

  • Serious illness, hospitalisation or medical emergency affecting the client, supported by documentation.
  • Bereavement in the immediate family.
  • Permanent relocation of the client to another city or country, supported by documentation.
  • Pregnancy, or a change in medical status that makes continuing treatment inadvisable.
  • Natural calamity, civil disruption, public health restrictions, or other events beyond reasonable control that prevent the clinic from delivering, or the client from receiving, the scheduled service.

In such cases, the clinic may offer a rescheduled appointment, an extension of package validity, clinic credit, transfer of the balance to an immediate family member, or a partial or full refund – whichever the clinic considers fair in the circumstances.

Any relaxation granted under this section is made on a goodwill basis, is decided case by case, and does not set a precedent or create an entitlement for any other client or any future request.
